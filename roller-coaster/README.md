# Energy on the Rails 2.0.0

Free roller coaster energy investigation for grades 6–8. Core TEKS 6.8B; NGSS MS-PS3-1 through student graphs and gravitational portion of MS-PS3-2 through a student system model.

Start at the station, follow a motor-powered lift, coast through adjustable hills and 0–2 loops, then brake. Choose 1–4 cars, 0–4 representative 35 kg riders per car, and empty car mass. Graphs and sensors reveal only visited data; the timeline revisits observed time. Existing buttons are retained.

Model: g=9.8 m/s²; lift releases at 4 m/s; PE sums each car’s rail-reference mgh; KE=½Mv². Resistance and brakes transfer energy to thermal. Captive rail and restraints are an idealization; body/rider center-of-mass offsets, wheel rotational energy, drag and ride-force safety analysis are omitted. An underpowered run stops at its first turning point.

English and draft Spanish; Spanish simulation and vocabulary cards await native science-educator review. Public cards support English, Spanish and bilingual print/PDF, CSV and offline HTML. The compatible teacher pack is version 2.0.0, delivered privately; paid files are not stored in this repository. Older 1.0.0 numeric keys are incompatible with the powered lift model.

Art uses supplied Sciesmic SVG assets with geometry-driven rails and bogies. Energy graphs and measurements are live model output. Shared kit unchanged.

Run the site root over HTTP. Production: https://labs.sciesmic.com/roller-coaster/
